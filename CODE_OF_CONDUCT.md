# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for
everyone. We act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

Examples of positive behavior: empathy and kindness, respect for differing opinions,
graceful acceptance of constructive feedback, focusing on what is best for the community.
Unacceptable: harassment, trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, publishing others'
private information, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate.

Report concerns to the maintainers at **foundry@lukasrepublic.com**. All reports are
reviewed promptly and fairly, respecting the reporter's privacy.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) v2.1.
